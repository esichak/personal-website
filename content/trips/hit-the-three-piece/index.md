Song of the ride: Just Can’t Get Enough -Black Eyed Peas

Finally hit the 3 piece at the flow trail (3 laps). After getting back from the last 3-4 weeks of travel, I completely forgot the days are getting shorter and showed up at 5:30 to find out sunrise isn’t until 6:30. Ended up climbing the fire road for 45 min using my iPhone flashlight and got to watch an awesome sunrise at the peak. I had a good spill on the first lap going otb over a berm. It probably looked like a sick roost for half a second before my rear tire regained traction and ejected me. Never going to do that fasted after a night of drinking also. Lots of lessons learned, but starting to get more comfortable transferring/maintaining speed through the downhills
