Song of the ride: “I Think I’m In Love” -Eddie Money

Sold the Marin on Tuesday and picked up the Nomad on Thursday. Couldn’t have timed that better if I tried. Going from 130/120 travel front/rear to 170/170 was quite noticeable in all the right ways. It somehow climbs just as well as my old trail bike with no noticeable bob. This thing brings new life to downhills, smart line choices become optional. Absolutely eats the tech and soaks up everything you hit. Still have to get used to the longer front end but I think that’ll come with some time as I get used to the bike. Running flats for the first few rides but can’t wait to go back to clips. Overall couldn’t be more stoked on the purchase
