Low avalanche danger and prime corn conditions made me want to try a big solo tour. I was a little nervous entering a new zone and actually got cliffed out on my first lap and had to boot pack back up. Once I got to the Y chute the snow was perfect. Had to haul back up for a second lap since the snow started getting sticky. Ended up joining another person for lap 2 and we got so lost trying to find the skin track to ski back down to the parking lot
