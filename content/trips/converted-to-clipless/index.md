First time riding the flow trail clipless and it was awesome! Not going to lie, I tipped over still clipped in on my driveway the first time I threw them on Wednesday. Got some practice after that and didn’t have any falls today. Love the extra control of the backend that the clips give you. Also got to test out my new GoPro and the videos are looking good. Still a lot of setting tweaks I need to play with but it’s pretty sweet so far. Also the clips felt so much more efficient, didn’t feel as much fatigue on the downhills and the ride overall wasn’t exhausting like it normally is.
