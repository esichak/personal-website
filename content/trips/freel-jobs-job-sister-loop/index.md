The “Tahoe triple crown” lol. Highest peak in Tahoe and it was a pretty cool link up between the three peaks. Surprisingly felt the elevation during the hike even though it didn’t break 11k. Knew it was going to be cold stepping out of the door with it being 32 degrees but the rime ice and fresh snow looked awesome. The cruz was the off-roading off 89 to get to the trailhead haha
