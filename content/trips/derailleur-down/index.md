First lap of the flow trail my derailleur comes off on segment 4. Had to ride down sticking my legs out seated but luckily 2 guys helped me get it back on after waking out a little at the bottom. Would have been a tough walk out if they hadn’t stopped to help me. Heat and humid was brutal but it felt like a fast ride with some PRs so can’t complain
