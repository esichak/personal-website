Everyone’s plan was to hike a few more miles and then to Paradise Valley Cafe for a meal. We saw the Jansport backpack with the Walmart bag attached to the outside of his pack guy but everyone is scared of him now because he told 2 girls he’d kill them. There are some strange people out here you just need to avoid. I had the same plan except everyone decided to take a zero in Idyllwild while I continued hiking after having lunch. I thought it was strange because there’s another storm coming in Saturday night with 90 mph gusts in the forecast. Having PTSD from the 2 days I was caught in a wind advisory in Laguna I’m pushing through over San Jacinto even though I could really use a zero. I hope to never get caught on a ridge having to camp in high wind again. What I anticipated being a nero (near zero mile day) ended up being over 20 miles again with lots of incline and I think the next 2 days should be pretty similar for me to beat the storm and get through Jacinto. I ended the day a little early taking an edible, setting up camp/cooking dinner, and then watching Survivor since I had service. Life on trail isn’t too bad rn.
