Cold night sleeping up at altitude but luckily my shoes and socks dried out overnight. Left camp around 6:30 and then had a super long climb down San Jacinto. In total the descent was over 7k ft which was tough on my calves and feet which were already hurting from the past few days. Still, I felt pretty solid and think my trail legs are really setting in. I’ve been lucky the past 13 days to not be in any pain but can feel certain muscles being tight and fatigued. During the climb down Jacinto had what looked like some awesome ski lines on its backside. Now I have to go read about it because it looked like it would be sweet during winter to ski down looking over Palm Springs. Finished Barbarian Days (my first audiobook) and while I enjoyed it, it wasn’t ideal to be listening to a surfing memoir when you’re going to be hiking for 5 months. At one point I took a step next to a bush and immediately heard a super loud rattle. It took me a second to register that it was a snake and after taking a quick glance into the bush I could see it coiling up. I jumped as high and far forward as I could and it was insane how loud the rattle was getting. I got a video right before it stopped so this was when it was quieting down but super scary and I was paranoid the entire way down after. Once I got down to the highway I called an Uber to head into town (Banning/Cabazon) to stay at a hotel through Monday. I have to wait until Monday morning to pick up my resupply package and this is a great excuse to take a zero day to recover and get out of the incoming wind storm. It was already getting pretty windy by midday so I was happy to be off Jacinto. Not it’s time for my first shower in 13 days. I’m filthy but you definitely get used to it. Felt bad for my Uber driver lol.
