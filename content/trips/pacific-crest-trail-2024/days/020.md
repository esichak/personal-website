After waking up, I decided to nero in Big Bear since my foot still was bruised and hurting plus the group of 3 I’m with are also heading in for a few days. It was a 3 mile trail down to Big Bear and from there we had a trail angel pick us up. My foot was already feeling better with the new lacing technique. On the way down I also finished my second audiobook Gone Girl. Super good listen and enjoyed his the book had a lot more to the ending compared to the movie. The trail angel that picked us up was super nice. She even went out of her way to give us an entire tour of the town. As we paid her she let us know she donates all the money to vets. Super nice lady. She dropped us off at a cafe and I got the polar bear which was their largest meal (eggs, hash browns, bacon, and two massive pancakes). I barely even touched the pancakes and had to take those to go for another meal. Then we left and walked around town before stopping at stater bros to buy 2 days worth of food. I ended up buying an IPA and enjoyed that with my pancakes in the parking lot before calling an Uber back to the trail. Along the way, we also stopped at a big 5 looking for stove fuel but all they had was 8oz cans which seemed like a little too much for the desert. I’ve now been cold soaking all my food for 10 days now and really don’t mind it. It was said to leave Joe, Poppins, and Little Chef but taking 3 zeros seemed like too much this early on. After getting back on the trail I hiked 4 miles back to the PCT and camped close to my spot from last night. My tent pitching skills have come a long way. It’s funny to think back to my first few nights and how loose my tent used to be.
