Last night was so cold it was the first time I woke up and had trouble falling back asleep. I might have to figure out how to add some heat for the sierras but heard desert mountains can be just as cold as the sierras. Even still I somehow slept past 6 for the first time and woke up at 7:30 so had a late start. It’s so nice now having plentiful water sources and not needing to do big water carries. Even with the extreme heat during the early afternoon, it’s a lot more manageable when you’re not carrying 11 lbs of water. I kept it a pretty low mileage day with my foot still feeling iffy but I think it’s getting better. Walking out of Big Bear and down to the valley reminds me a lot of the drive up here we used to do when skiing. The huge brown rocks and super unique and made for some cool scenery with snowy peaks in the background. Hoping to get back to 20+ miles days tomorrow so let’s see how it goes.
