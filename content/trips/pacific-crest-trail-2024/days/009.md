Big day today. Started it off by hiking 2 miles into Warner Springs to pick up my 2nd resupply box and steal some TP. Luckily they opened at 8 so I was able to organize and charge up my battery packs for 2 hours. Then Lucy from Australia and I hitchhiked back to the trail in a van which it turns out had people inside also hiking the PCT. After that, it was a little over 18 miles in some intense heat with constant incline. I messed up the last water source and overshot it right before the intense uphill section with heat and no clean water. It was a good 8 miles with only half a liter of water but luckily I met Julia from Germany who was able to give me a liter. Not sure if I would have made it through the day without that. We also passed a trail family of 10 people and Julia was not happy about it lol. Later I kicked plant that looked like an agave plant but the spikes at the end poked through my shoes/socks and broke off. I had to take off everything to pick out each one which was deep and painful. Longest and farthest day yet. Finished it off by getting to Mike’s Place around 7. It’s a ranch that’s marked as a water source but I guess he lets hikers come onto his ranch and camp out. Sometimes he’s here but not tonight. Might be good since people say he’s a pretty weird guy. Kind of sounds like the intro plot to a horror movie or something. Shoutout Mike because there are lots of heads here and not many campsites around. Water has been pretty unreliable so tomorrow should be another interesting day.
