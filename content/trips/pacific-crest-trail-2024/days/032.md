After the super long day yesterday, I didn’t think much about trying to pitch my tent under a tree since we were right by the stream so when I woke up my tent and quilt were soaked. Everyone else had the same problem which was a bummer because it was such a nice campsite. I had a decent climb right from the start but I can really feel now that my shoes are dead and no longer have much support or cushion. Around noon I walked towards a dip in the trail and heard a rattlesnake in a bush right next to the trail. I ended up having to slide down the hill on the other side and walk around because it wouldn’t stop rattling right along the trail. After that, I had to stop to air our tent and quilt which was annoying but hopefully, I’ll figure out condensation soon. I can feel each day getting hotter and hotter now as we enter the true desert. There was also a beehive I passed and then got swarmed by bees as I had to run away. Hit the 500 mile marker shortly before getting to camp which has a very steep hike down to a stream which I saw multiple people slip out on. Few mosquitoes also which I forgot was part of the trail since bugs haven’t been bad so far. Tomorrow we head into Hikertown before doing the LA aqueduct at night.
