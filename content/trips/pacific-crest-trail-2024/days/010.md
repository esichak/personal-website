Started off the day super early leaving Mike's place at 6:30 to try to get ahead of the heat. Other groups left at 3 and 4:30 which with hindsight might have been the call. It ended up being a very long hot day with terrible water sources. Luckily I cared 3 liters from mikes place but the only other water sources were a tiny stream that looked like runny shit with no flow and a cistern that had green water and bugs all over. Everyone was also saying that a snake was living in the cistern and the only way to get water was to reach into the dark hole. So it ended up being my second day in a row messing up water and I was dying in the heat during the afternoon out of water. I joined poppins and queen bee for the last section to help power through down to a hostel which was actually just somebody’s house. It was very nice of them but I also can’t imagine having 15-20 hikers a day show up and camp in your backyard. Not complaining though except the backyard wasn’t flat and I kept sliding off my mattress all night. Even still it was worth it for the water alone. I also kicked a Chaparral Yucca cactus the day before and found out that I missed one of the spikes and it’s now stuck in my foot with skin grown over it. From what I’ve read online I should be fine but it’s saying the pain might last for weeks which isn’t ideal having it be on the outside of my foot. As long as it doesn’t get infected I’ll be happy.
