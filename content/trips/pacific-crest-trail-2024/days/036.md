Taking a zero day today in Mojave and didn’t do too much aside from laundry and going to the grocery store. Mojave isn’t that nice of a place and there’s not much to do around here. I was surprised the laundry was only $2 but my shirt came out just as dirty and my shorts ripped a hole in the bottom. After that, I went to stater bros to get food for the next 2 days. It’s only 83 today but with no wind, the heat is still pretty intense. Glad there’s been some wind the past few days for me in the desert. The bottoms of my feet are still pretty sore so I’m debating if I need to get some new shoes or insoles before Kennedy Meadows South but this is probably my last chance to for the next 150 miles.
