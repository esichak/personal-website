Pretty cold night last night up at elevation but today we’re heading into Big Bear. I used to ski here in college so it was fun to come back to the cool area with great scenery along with the ski resort you can see across the lake.

I passed a couple that said they were recovering from norovirus so I got ahead of them and pushed on so they couldn’t catch up. They said it was terrible and they’d quit the trail if they got it again. I’m a little conflicted right now between going into Big Bear or pushing it to my next resupply in Cajon. It’s a little stressful allocating food for each day and planning resupplys. I am low on food but going into Big Bear would take up most of the day. I’m camping right next to the pull off so I’ll see how I’m feeling tomorrow morning. The outside middle of my foot is really hurting and bruised so that’ll be the determining factor. I texted blaze physio and she recommended a lacing technique so let’s see how that works tomorrow.
