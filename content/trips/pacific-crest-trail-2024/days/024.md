Short few miles to start the day into Cajon where I stopped for McDonald’s and my resupply package. I ate breakfast at McDonald’s, walked to the Cajon In to get my resupply box, and then went back for lunch before heading back out to the trail. In a gas station parking lot, I started talking to a family that wanted to follow my hike on Instagram and had another guy ask how many people were on my team which I thought was funny. Heading out I had a big climb and water carry ahead. I loaded up 5 liters from the water cache and my pack was heavy with 5 days of food. As I was getting water I watched a big rattlesnake go over someone’s iPhone right next to their pack. The entire afternoon was a climb. I only got halfway through the climb before setting up camp and I’m glad I did since there weren’t a ton of campsites around and they filled up quickly.
