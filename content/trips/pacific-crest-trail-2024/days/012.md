Ended up sleeping on another slope last night and slept terribly. Every time I would turn over my sleeping bag would slide down the pad and I’d have to pull myself back up. I got an early start leaving camp a little after 6 because I wasn’t sure if I’d need to get through any snow before it melted too much. We’re right in the middle of the transition period from needing microspikes for the snow up here and not. I didn’t bring them so hoping there’s not too much snow. While I didn’t cross a ton of snow today there were so many blown down trees. Some parts felt like an obstacle course to get through. Feeling the past 3 20+ mile days I ended the day early again. The next 2 days should be one final push through San Jacinto before I can take a zero. Hopefully the snow isn’t too bad over the summit and it doesn’t get too cold at night since I’m camping at 9k. At camp I heard there was another rescue today at a spring I had stopped at for water. Super weird since he got airlifted off Jacinto for dehydration when the spot he was at had lots of water. I’ve been hearing of a lot of rescues here lately. I also got worried I was getting an ingrown toenail but I think it might just be my first blister close to my toenail. I was freaking out about it after reading online but think I just need to put some lueko tape over it.
