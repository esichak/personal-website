Felt pretty fatigued at the start of today probably from the big day and heat yesterday. It was surprisingly windy which I’m never a fan of but walking along Silverwood Lake had great views. After passing the lake it was a big climb before setting up camp. I could hear the trains passing nearby but it was super windy and I was luckily able to find a sheltered spot in a dry river bed. It was satisfying to hear the wind gusts above me while I was completely protected from the wind. Foot is feeling a lot better now which is good because there’s another tough section coming up.
