Pretty short hike to start the morning into Hikertown with only 9 miles all downhill. Walking downhill I could see the giant section of desert ahead, completely flat before the next and final mountain of the Southern California section. Right as I got to the road I ran into Peter and Moonshine along with a few other people I have met in the past. We all hitched to Wee Vill Market with Peter and I sitting in the bed of the trunk. After eating a burger there we went back to a closer market to buy beer before going back to Hikertown to hang out. There Shmutz’s dad met up with her and bought a few cases of beer for everyone so we spent the afternoon drinking in the shade at Hikertown which was a super interesting place. Each town building was a room people could book or you could pitch your tent for free. There were probably 50 hikers there all waiting out the heat before night hiking the LA aqueduct. Peter and I ended up leaving at 5. Loaded up with 4 liters of water I ended up only hiking until 10 before I found a nice sheltered tent spot for the night. The weather will be cool tomorrow so I don’t feel the need to hike throughout the night and mess up my sleep schedule. I got to night hike for 2 hours which was fun but I was glad I pitched my tent when I did because it was completely sheltered from the wind which was picking up. I’ll have another 10 mile water carry tomorrow morning but it shouldn’t be bad with the cooler weather.
