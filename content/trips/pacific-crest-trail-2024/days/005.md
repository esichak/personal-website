Today was miserable. Aside from 10 hours of sleep, I woke up to a soaking tent and heavy winds. Within 10 minutes of hiking I was soaked with it being 38 degrees and getting hit with 30 mph winds with 45 mph gusts. The gust were so strong I had to stop hiking and brace some almost pushing me over. It made the first 4 hours of hiking not fun. Constantly being hit with gusts of wind while I’m cold and wet. Luckily the sun came out around 1 but the wind has been consistent. I also had a good scare as I was setting up camp with not much daylight left. At my initial spot I spent 30 min trying to pitch my tent in heavy winds before realizing the wind was too strong for my tent. I ended up hiking another mile to find a better spot and was luckily able to squeeze into a more protected spot. Wind is still howling but the weather app is saying it should clear by 1am so let’s hope tomorrow’s a better day. Oh ya this was all while I was carrying 6 liters of water for the last 4 miles since there’s no water for the next 24 miles.
