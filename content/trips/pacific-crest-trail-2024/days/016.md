Slow morning since the Cabazon post office didn’t open until 8:30. After packing my food bag there I ordered another Uber to the trail and got started around 9:30. The day simultaneously flew by but also felt really long and slow. I wasn’t feeling great the entire day and I think it’s probably due to the junk I ate yesterday. Donuts, Mexican food, and some other fast food. I thought I needed the calories but lesson learned to not totally pig out on zero days. It didn’t help I was now carrying 5 days of food in my pack. Along with that, I’m also in a section where people think a stream has norovirus so midday I packed out 5 liters so that I can be fine on water through tomorrow afternoon and not drink the sketchy water. At least the terrain today was pretty unique. There was one section I walked by a rattlesnake that was stretched out and didn’t even realize it was there until I had passed it. It was less than a foot away from the trail and didn’t move so I wasn’t sure if it was alive but after poking it with my pole it immediately started rattling super loud and coiling up. I quickly left and continued on. After getting away from the interstate I climbed over a mountain and down into this valley filled with sand that had multiple streams running through it. I only had to cross one of the streams but it’s known as socal's biggest one and it had a decently fast flow up to my knees but it wasn’t too challenging with my poles. The section I’m doing tomorrow washed out from Hurricane Hillary so I think it’ll be a pretty tricky day hiking and navigating. Planning to have a mellow night and start up early tomorrow.
