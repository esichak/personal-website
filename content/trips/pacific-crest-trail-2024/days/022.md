Wow 3 weeks on trail now. Really doesn’t feel like it. Still feels like I’m just a few days in getting started which is why I’m glad I’m documenting this all here. Got an early start to the day waking up at 5:45 and getting on trail quickly. By mid morning I reached mile marker 300. Later on, I passed Deep Creek Hot Springs. Since it was a Sunday it was pretty busy there so people were partying and even had a slackline setup. I chose not to get in the hot springs since it looked really crowded and I was already super hot. The heat was intense as I hiked most of the day in a canyon. Even though it was hot it was bearable with all of the water sources along the way. I never had to carry more than 1.5 liters. I think I’ve reached the point where I can trust the water sources and no longer need to worry about norovirus. At one point in the shade, I almost stepped on a rattlesnake but caught it right as I was about to put my foot down. I leaped over it and it didn’t even start to rattle but still a close call. As I exited the canyon and still had 5 miles left before camp there was some trail magic in a parking lot. They had cold drinks and fruit so I had a Mountain Dew and cuties. I can’t remember the last time I’ve had Mountain Dew but it gave me the energy boost I needed to make it to camp. My foot felt really good today but let’s see how it is tomorrow morning after a 20+ mile day. It’s funny out here how aches and pains can really impact you mentally. Luckily I haven’t had many but once you feel something come on you immediately catastrophize it thinking about the worst case scenario and how it could potentially take you off trail. Then a few days later you get over it and feel unstoppable. Let’s hope my foot is better now and I can continue on back to normal.
