Left camp a little earlier today at 6:30 since it’s going to be a tough day to navigate. A group of 3 camped by me last night and we made plans to hike together since the PCTA put out a notice to skip this section due to storm damage but a few people I talked to said it was fine as long as you knew to for the creek for the first 10 miles and stay off the trail. Joe, poppins, little chef, and I headed out and ended up crossing the creek 42 times so our feet were soaked the entire day. As we walked through the creek it almost looked like it was snowing with the pollen coming down. The creek was super cool to walk through and had a bunch of sand. After 10 miles we joined back up with the PCT and had another 10 miles of washed out trail climbing a total of 5.5k throughout the day. The entire day was technical, steep, tough to navigate, and slow going. We made it to camp late and crashed right as the sunset. This creek is what everyone thinks the norovirus outbreak is from so luckily I was able to borrow some chemical water treatment drops from them.
