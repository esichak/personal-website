Finally made it over San Jacinto. Started off the day pretty late leaving camp at 7:30 which was a mistake because by the time I reached the snow, it was already pretty soft. I didn’t expect over three quarters of today to be hiking on top of snow and really wish I had microspikes with me. There were some pretty sketchy ridges traversing on snow where a slip or fall would have been pretty consequential. On top of that the trail was extremely hard to follow. Footpaths were hard to see in the snow and sometimes didn’t follow the map I was using on my phone. I have no clue how people hiked this pre phone navigation. Definitely the scariest hiking I’ve done outside of Half Dome. But the views were amazing and the whole morning I was looking at a peak across from Jacinto that looked perfect for spring skiing. So many different couloirs had me mind skiing them as I hiked along. Would be cool to come back and ski them in the future. It ended up being a super long day taking 10 hours to only do 16 miles. Towards the end, I was dropping into the snow past my knees and it wasn’t fun. My shoes and socks were soaked for the last 4 miles and I just wanted the day to be done but my only option was to keep going since the ground was covered in snow and there was nowhere to camp. Luckily I should be done with the snow here and that’s the most technical section of the socal segment so relieved to make it through. At camp right now and all I want is a warm meal but I just ran out of fuel for my stove…
