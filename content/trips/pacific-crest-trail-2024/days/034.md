I realized my tent spot last night was pretty great as I continued on the LA aqueduct and didn’t see many other sites with wind protection. I started a little late for the desert but the weather is super nice being cool with a decent breeze. This section is covered with windmills. The first half of the day was pretty flat and then I started the final Southern California section’s climb where the wind really picked up with no protection or shade. There was also a crazy amount of grasshoppers all around jumping up right as I was about to step on them. Overall the weather was perfect today staying relatively cool for a section that can be the hottest part of the entire trail. The winds have been strong tonight so let’s hope my tent stays up and they die down.
