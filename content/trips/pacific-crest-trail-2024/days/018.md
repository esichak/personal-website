Hiked today with the same group of 3 as yesterday. We had a 16 mile water carry so we started early but it was an easier day since there wasn’t much climbing. It was a super cold morning and we came across some more snow patches to cross but nothing like Jacinto. After getting down San Gorgonio we headed towards Big Bear. Even though we took it easy and slow today we were still able to put in 20 miles. Right before lunch, we passed mile marker 250. Unfortunately, this fun group is taking a double zero in Big Bear so I think I’ll be pushing on to El Cajon for my next resupply. It might be a big push and might be tight with my remaining food.
