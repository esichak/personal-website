Early start today leaving camp at 6:30 to catch the free bus from Highway 58 overpass to Mojave for a double zero. Didn’t want to double zero but with the post office closed for Memorial Day I’ll now be stuck in Mojave for another day. The wind luckily died last night after the sunset but that was some of the strongest wind on the trail yet (excluding the Mount Laguna storm). Some people I talked to were saying some people's tents ripped from the wind but this morning there’s a nice calm breeze. The entire day was walking through one of the largest wind farms in the world so the views weren’t great but at least they were pretty unique with windmills all around you. I timed the bus pretty well and got to the overpass at 4 for the 4:30 ride. Mojave isn’t the nicest area but it’s cheap and has a grocery store so can’t complain. My feet hurt but didn’t realize how consistently big the past few days have been plus my shoes are packed out with 500+ miles on them. Hoping the double zero can help them recover and get these shoes to KMS at mile marker 700.
