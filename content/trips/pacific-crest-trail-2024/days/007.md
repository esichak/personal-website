So I heard from a few people who shuttled from Julian today that there was a wind advisory put out yesterday which makes sense and explains my troubles and misery but I like to think that’s the toughest tent pitching conditions I’ll face on trail. Also explains why I haven’t seen anyone in the last 24 hours because everyone I saw today came from a Julian shuttle. All things considered I actually slept pretty well even with the wind pounding my tent throughout the night. Halfway through the water carry there was an unexpected water cache under a bridge which was painful to still have 4 liters in my pack and not be able to use any of that water. Rest of the day was hot but not too bad and it ended at another water cache with a super nice secluded tent spot.
