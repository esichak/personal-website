Garmin wasn’t working at the start of today for some reason. The actual stats were 9.6 miles and 2.4k of elevation gain with a 3.1k descent. We continued to walk through a few avalanche paths which I find super interesting and cool to look at. From our campsite, we had a short hike along the PCT before taking an 8 mile side trail into town. The side trail was a decent climb up over Kearsarge Pass followed by a long steep climb down. The altitude is still tough even when you think you’re acclimated being at or above 11k altitude past 3 days. After we made it down we hitchhiked from the trailhead parking lot into Independence where I picked up a resupply package from the post office before hitchhiking up to Bishop for a zero tomorrow. The ride ended up being 45 minutes but the hitch didn’t take too long to get. In Bishop it was 95 in town with 11 UV and so hot. We were very relieved to not be hiking and couldn’t imagine how miserable to people still hiking in the desert are. After checking into our hotel room we got McDonald’s for a late lunch quickly before doing laundry. I was the only one without town clothes so I had to walk around town in my rain gear and was so hot/sweating but there were some cool gear stores where I bought a gravity water filter attachment, a new toothbrush, and a replacement trowel since I just lost mine yesterday. After laundry, it was pretty late so we went to a Mexican restaurant where I had my first wet burrito. Then we went to a bar to play pool but the table wasn’t level, it was a weird place, and we were exhausted so we ended leaving after the first game.
