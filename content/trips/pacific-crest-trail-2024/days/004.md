Last night was tough. Sub 40 temps, rain, and heavy wind. Woke up freezer with a soaked tent but luckily it was only a 2 mile hike into mount laguna for some hot breakfast and my resupply box. During those 2 miles we got passed by an older guy wearing a hoodie and jeans with a jansport backpack with a Walmart duffle bag and gallon jug attached to the outside. As we talked to him he also pulled out a bottle of whiskey and said he’s going to cowboy camp the entire trail. Post office didn’t open until noon so I had to book it after that since there’s a 12 mile no camping zone I had to get through. Pretty brutal day not being able to get warm and getting hammered by 30 mph winds all day that felt like they could almost knock me over. Assuming tonight’s going to be rough again and then the cold front should pass tomorrow hopefully
