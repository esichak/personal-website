Adam forgot his poles but luckily someone in the lot had an extra pair. Started up Dunderberg with a straight 2k boot up with crampons. Super firm on the way up and then it was borderline too soft by 10am. After making it back to the car at 11 we shot up Mount black and then scrambled to the east face. So much cool access here with the lot being at 10k
