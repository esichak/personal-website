Well the rave must be a festival because they were still going at 6:15 this morning. On my second lap I joined a group on their first lap and they said the rave was still going on their climb. As I led on the second lap I had a pretty solid fall going pretty fast. Luckily I had a few feet straight ahead to fall but took a good impact to my left shoulder. Just a few scratches and bruises but shirt isn’t ripped so it could have been a lot worse. Not the most confidence inspiring ride to finish with flats on, but looking forward to switching to clipless
