Got on the fire road at 5:50 am and 5 minutes into the climb I pass a full on rave in the soquel mountains. Not my first choice of climbing music but the tempo had me keeping a good cadence on the 30 min climb. Ran into another solo biker and ended up doing the 2 flow trail laps with him. First one I got to follow and try to keep up and second one he let me lead. First time doing segment 1 non stop and segment 2 and 3 together. Super nice dude though and he was giving me a lot of body position/technique tips which was awesome. The progression continues
